❓ How to use the Logistics dashboard

One place for every tour, driver, schedule and Vicki's backfill master. Hover most buttons for a tip. Here's each tab:

📈 Overview

At-a-glance KPIs (tours, staffed %, drivers needed, maintenance, drive miles), tours by business line and by Account Exec, and the "why convert" before/after.

📊 Master Schedule

View by AE / Driver / Director / Business, as a 🗓 Calendar (zoom slider + ‹ › to scroll) or a ☰ List. Search any tour, or filter to needs driver / maintenance. Click a tour for its day-by-day schedule; click the ✎ to edit / move / delete; ＋ Add tour to add. 🖨 Print a report grouped by AE / Director / Business.

🚚 Fleet & Tours

The full roster as a table, grouped by AE / Director / Business — tour, asset, driver, miles, maintenance.

🏠 Arriving / Maintenance

Trailers due back at MRA, sorted by date, with maintenance/issues flagged red — for shop capacity planning.

👤 Backfill (Vicki)

Vicki's real master calendar, month by month (‹ ›). Filter by driver or text, or show only APL. The open-gap finder lists who's free a given week. Click a day's ＋ to add an entry; click any entry to edit / move (change the date) / delete. 🖨 Print the month (calendar or list) or the full year.

📋 Run of Show

Opens on New report: fill the form → Generate builds the report in MRA format. The address auto-maps a parking / staging pin (OpenStreetMap) — click the map to drop the exact spot — plus an Open in Google Maps link. 📄 Samples shows examples. 🖨 Print / PDF to share.

📒 Driver Log

Drivers log each day — location, drive hours, generator hours, miles. Running totals up top, filter by driver. Feeds utilization + the Affiliation Report timesheet.

✎ Editing

Add / edit / move / delete works on the Master and Vicki tabs. This is a prototype — edits show instantly but are in-memory (a refresh resets them); a real save is a later phase.
