The play: On the next vacancy for a small/entry suite, list it at $325–$350/wk instead of $300. Do NOT raise rates on current tenants mid-lease — only apply to new sign-ons.

Why it works: Sola's entry suite is estimated ~$265–$290/wk with no concierge, no free laundry, no coffee/water service. A $325–$350 entry rate is still $40–$60 below Sola's estimated top rate and deeply justified by your amenity stack.

Risk: Low. If a suite sits vacant 3+ weeks at $325, drop to $310 for that lease. You've tested the ceiling.

2 5% Annual Renewal Escalator in All New Leases
Built-in rate growth without renegotiating every year +$29K–$38K/yr
The play: Update your lease template today: add a clause stating that upon annual renewal, the weekly rate increases by 5% (or a fixed dollar amount like $20/wk). This is standard in commercial real estate and professional tenants expect it.

Why it works: At an average rate of $400/wk, a 5% escalator = +$20/wk per suite. Across all 38 suites renewing over 12–24 months, this compounds significantly. It also anchors the expectation that rates rise — tenants plan for it rather than being surprised.

Action this week: Add one sentence to your lease renewal clause. No cost, no friction, massive upside.

3 Create a Premium Corner / Window Suite Tier
Identify your 6–8 best suites and price them separately +$18K–$30K/yr
The play: Walk your building and tag every suite with a window, corner location, larger square footage, or exceptional natural light. List these as a distinct "Premium Suite" tier at $75–$100/wk above base. Create a separate section on your listing page for these.

Why it works: Stylists pay a premium for natural light — it's a core part of their client experience and Instagram-able results. You can charge more for these without resistance because the value is visible and visceral. None of your competitors are doing tiered pricing publicly.

Bonus: Premium suites create a visible upgrade path for existing tenants ("when suite 14 opens, you can move up for $75 more").

4 Lead Every Ad & Listing with the Concierge
Zero competitors in Rochester Hills offer full-time concierge Faster Lease-Up
The play: In every Google ad, Salonrenter.com listing, Instagram post, and Vagaro profile — the FIRST line should be: "Rochester Hills' only salon suite with a full-time concierge." Not a footnote. The headline.

Why it works: A stylist choosing between you and Sola doesn't just want a room — they want support. Also mention: Free coffee, tea, water bottles, and ice for all clients — this is a client experience differentiator that helps stylists retain their own clients. Lead with it on social.

5 Counter beautiLofts "8 Weeks Free" Promo Smartly
Beat their offer with higher perceived value, lower cost to you Protect Occupancy
The play: beautiLofts is offering 8 weeks free rent (~$1,400–$2,560 giveaway at their rates). Instead of matching dollar-for-dollar, counter with a bundle that costs you less but feels more premium: "2 weeks free + free Vagaro Professional setup ($299 value) + free first month's coffee/beverage service."

Why it's better: Their promo is just a discount. Yours is an experience upgrade. The Vagaro setup alone saves the stylist 4–6 hours of onboarding time. The hospitality bundle shows them day-one what the concierge environment feels like. Total cost to you: ~$600 vs their $1,400–$2,560.

When to use: Only activate this counter-promo when you have 3+ vacant suites simultaneously. Don't discount when full.

7 Tenant Retention Check-In Program
Every turnover costs 2–6 weeks of vacancy Save $5K–$14K/yr
The play: 60 days before each lease renewal, the concierge does a brief check-in: "Hey [name], your renewal is coming up — is there anything we can do to make the space work better for you?" This surfaces problems before they become move-outs.

Why it matters: A vacancy at $400/wk costs you $800–$2,400 for every 2–6 weeks it takes to re-lease. At 38 suites with natural turnover, reducing just 3 unnecessary move-outs per year saves $2,400–$7,200 in lost rent plus leasing friction.

Bonus: Tenants who feel heard stay longer, move up to premium suites, and refer other stylists — the three biggest revenue multipliers in your business.

Never let competitors close the gap without knowing it Stay Ahead

Why it matters: The current data in this dashboard (June 2026) will be stale within 6 months. Rates move. beautiLofts ran an 8-week free promo — that's a sign of vacancy pressure. Trigger rule: If your occupancy drops below 80% for 4+ weeks → immediately check what competitors are doing and whether a promo is needed.
