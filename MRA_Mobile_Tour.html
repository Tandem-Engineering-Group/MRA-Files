How to use the MRA Mobile Tour Schedule

The replacement for MyMobileTour — built fresh and owned by you. Three tabs across the top: Tour Schedule, Equipment Hours & Miles, and Misc — TBD.

📊 The top tiles (at a glance)

  • Active Tours — tours with stops still upcoming.
  • Upcoming Stops — stops from today forward.
  • Stops Today, Total Mileage, Generator Hrs, Equipment In Service — running totals (mileage/generator fill in as hours are logged).

🗓 Tour Schedule — getting around

  • Calendar / List toggle (top right) — month grid, or a clean day-by-day list.
  • ‹ › moves between months; Today jumps back to now (today's date is outlined in red).
  • View specific tour dropdown — pick one tour to see just its route; choose All tours to reset.
  • Every tour has its own color; the legend under the calendar shows what's what for the month.

➕ Adding & editing stops

  • ＋ Add Event (top right) opens a blank stop. Or hover a day and click its little ＋ to add on that date.
  • Click any stop on the calendar or list to edit it — tour, date, status, city, state, venue, crew, equipment, notes.
  • Pick an existing tour from the dropdown, or choose ➕ New tour… to start one.
  • Save applies it instantly; Delete (bottom-left of the editor) removes a stop.

🖨 Printing

Hit 🖨 Print…, then choose a quick range (This month, Next 30 days, This week, Everything) or set your own From / To dates. It opens a clean, printer-friendly schedule grouped by day — print it or save as PDF.

🚚 Equipment Hours & Miles

This is the old Affiliation Report.

  • ＋ Add Entry logs labor hours, vehicle mileage, and generator runtime against a tour, person and piece of equipment.
  • Equipment list — add/edit your trucks (tractors), vehicles, and generators.
  • Crew — manage the roster of names that show up for assignments and logs.

🌙 / ☀️ Light & dark mode

The moon/sun button in the header flips the whole board between dark and light; it remembers your choice on that device.

🧩 Misc — TBD

Placeholders for the leftover MyMobileTour modules (Documents, Hotel List, Map View, etc.). Tell us which to build out; the rest get dropped.

💾 Saving & sharing

The board lives at a shareable web link — anyone you send it to can view and explore it, in their browser, on any device.

While it's a prototype: your edits save to your browser only — they're a live preview, not yet shared with the team or saved permanently. Making edits shared & permanent is the next step (Save → Power Automate → Excel, the same way the shop board works).

About the data: the schedule was imported from the June–October 2026 calendars (156 stops). Each date comes from its own month's calendar, so overlap days are counted once. Equipment, crew and logged hours are sample placeholders until the real lists are added.
