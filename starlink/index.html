📘 User Guide

Quick Start

  1. Section 1 — pick your Primary Location (US or Canada). This sets currency and handling fee.
  2. Section 2 — choose a plan. By default, enter your known monthly usage; or switch to "Use Daily Calc × Days/Mos" to estimate from streaming + user behavior.
  3. Review Total Monthly Cost and the Suggested Plan callout.
  4. Section 3 — set Months of Service for the annual projection.
  5. Section 4 — pick your hardware kit and optional trailer mount for the one-time sell price.

How the Daily Data Calculator Works

Daily GB = (Video Mbps × Video Hrs × 3600 ÷ 8 ÷ 1024) + (Active Users × Hrs/User × 3600 × 1.5 ÷ 8 ÷ 1024).

  • The 1.5 Mbps assumption covers browsing/email/apps per active user.
  • Common bitrates: SD=3, HD=5, FHD=8, 4K=25 Mbps.
  • Daily GB × Days/Mos = Monthly GB used in the plan calculation.

Plan Selection Tips

  • Local Priority — cheapest, in-region use.
  • Global Priority — international roaming / remote.
  • Overage is billed in 50GB blocks.
  • The Suggested Plan callout picks the cheapest plan whose bandwidth covers your usage.

Hardware

Handling Fees

  • US plans: +$50/month

STARLINK PRICING CHEAT SHEET

For MRA Sales Team — Quick Reference
Effective May 1, 2026 · v3.1
A. The Basics
📅Plans bill on the 1st of each month
💵Customer bill = Starlink plan + MRA handling fee
📦Hardware = one-time sell price (kit + optional mount)
B. How Monthly Pricing Works
BASE PLAN + HANDLING FEE = MONTHLY BILL
Base plan (US): $55+ (starts at 50GB plan)
Handling fee: $50 (US) · $75 (CAD)
MRA does NOT mark up the Starlink service cost. The flat handling fee covers monitoring and billing on the customer's behalf — managing data caps, top-ups, plan changes, and invoices.

The customer always pays Starlink's published rate, plus our handling fee. Pricing is transparent.
C. When the Data Runs Out — 3 Options
🛑
Hard Stop
Service cuts off the moment the data cap is reached. No service until the 1st of next month.
✓ Fixed monthly cost
Best for: clients with strict budgets
🔄
Auto Top-Up
Starlink automatically adds another 50GB block at the plan's overage rate. Continues all month, resets on the 1st.
⚠ Variable cost · No credit for unused data
Best for: clients who need uninterrupted service
📦
Higher Starting Bucket
Start the customer on a larger plan (e.g., 500GB or 1TB) for a fixed, predictable monthly cost.
✓ Fixed cost · No credit for unused data
Best for: clients who need budget predictability
D. Pausing Service
  • Service can be paused at any time through Starlink
  • Billing runs from the 1st of the month — paused service still runs through the remainder of the current month
  • Service does not auto-reactivate — the client must resume manually
  • When resumed mid-month, the monthly fee is prorated for the remaining days
EXAMPLE TIMELINE
Mar  1▸Billed for March
Mar 12▸Paused (service active until Mar 31)
Apr  1▸No charge (paused all of April)
Apr 16▸Resumed
Apr 16▸Prorated: 15 days of April
May  1▸Full monthly fee resumes
E. Quick Answers for Sales
Q: Do we mark up the service?
A: No — flat handling fee only ($50 US / $75 CAD).
Q: Can we refund unused data?
A: No — data does not roll over or refund.
Q: Does the bill change month to month?
A: Only if Auto Top-Up is enabled. Hard Stop and Higher Bucket are fixed.
Q: Can a client pause mid-month?
A: Yes — service runs through the rest of the month they pause.
