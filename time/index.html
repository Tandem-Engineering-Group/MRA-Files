      🕒 How to log your time

      Do this for each job you work on. Takes 10 seconds.

      1. Tap your name at the top (Employee).
      2. Pick the job — start typing the job number or name, then tap it in the list.
      3. Enter your hours — type them, or tap a quick number: ½   1   2   4   8   10.
      4. Tap “＋ Add time entry.” Done — it saves right away.
      5. Worked more than one job? Just repeat for the next one.

      👀 Check the “Your week” box under the form — it shows everything you’ve added for the whole week, grouped by day with a day total and a week total. Add time for Tuesday and Wednesday shows up right alongside Monday's, so by Friday you can see your whole week in one place.
