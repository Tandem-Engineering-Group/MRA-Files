Bulletproof Logistical Services

Maintenance, repair & operations for mobile medical units

Already own a medical trailer? MRA keeps it reliable, compliant and on-site. Start with the Essential Package; layer on-demand repair and full vehicle operations on top when your program needs the road handled too.

Our Solution

Two packages. One reliable unit.

Your clinical team should never have to think about tires, generators or permits — treatment time should never be lost to downtime.

Essential Maintenance Package

Semi-annual preventative maintenance inspections — every system reviewed and documented

  • Tires — proper inflation, cuts, tread depth and wear
  • Generator inspection — filter, oil, plugs, belts and more
  • Electrical system test — shore power inspection
  • Axle bearings — wear and fluid levels
  • Trailer HVAC systems — filters, coil cleaning, freon & pressure levels
  • Plumbing & water systems — pumps, drainage, holding tanks, filtration
  • Brakes — pads, discs, drums, lines and all associated components
  • Hydraulics — operation, leaks and fluid levels
  • Slide rails — cleaned & maintained per manufacturer specs
  • Rubber seals — expandables, door frames, belly boxes, windows
  • Annual DOT inspection — of the trailer, documented

On-Demand Service & Operational Package

Pairs with the Essential Package — repairs on demand plus full turn-key vehicle operations

On-Demand Repair Services
  • HVAC system
  • Electrical systems
  • Plumbing systems
  • Cabinetry, furnishings & flooring
Operational Services
  • MRA CDL-A driver + all associated driver expenses (lodging, per diem)
  • Fuel for the vehicle
  • Insurance
  • Permit management — across jurisdictions
  • Appropriate tow vehicle — tractor etc., if required
  • Storage between locations — for multi-day transport
  • Structural & mechanical inspection + full transport prep before departure
  • Delivery & placement — at the host site location

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you service units you didn't build?
Yes. Our maintenance and repair packages cover mobile medical trailers and coaches of any make — chassis, generator, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, slide systems and seals, fully documented.
Can you move a unit between provinces?
That's our specialty. The operational package includes an MRA CDL-A driver, the right tow vehicle, fuel, insurance, permit management across jurisdictions, storage between locations, full transport prep and placement at the host site.
How often are maintenance inspections?
The Essential Package reviews every system semi-annually, plus the annual DOT inspection of the trailer — with documentation your compliance team can file.
We don't own a unit — can we still run a mobile program?
Yes — see Units & Programs. We lease fully customizable medical trailers with an operational program sized to your mandate.
What does a quote look like?
Itemized: the unit (if leased), transport legs, maintenance schedule and operations — line by line, built for health-system budgeting and approvals.
Do you work through provincial procurement portals?
Yes — we respond to public tenders and work with shared-services organizations and health-authority procurement teams, and we can supply the documentation those processes require (insurance, safety records, DOT/CVOR compliance).
What happens in winter?
Canadian winters are exactly why the maintenance program exists: generator load, HVAC, seals and plumbing are inspected and winterized so clinics keep operating through the season — and storage between deployments is part of the operational package.
Can you store our unit between seasons?
Yes. Secure storage between locations and between seasons is included in the operational package, with a full structural and mechanical inspection before the unit goes back on the road.
