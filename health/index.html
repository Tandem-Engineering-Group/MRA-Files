MRA Healthcare Canada — three website concepts

Three complete, working homepage designs for the Canadian mobile-medical business, all built from the brand assets (logos, one-pager, site mock-up) and the same core content — services, packages, "Easy as 1-2-3", and the trusted-by list. Click a tab above or a card below to browse each one full-screen. The Market Research tab is a briefing on the Canadian mobile-healthcare opportunity with sources.

Internal preview only — not a public site. Forms are visual mock-ups (not wired). Phone number, services, packages and client names come from the MRA Healthcare one-pager and mock-up. Domain on the table: mrahealthcare.ca (see the research tab for a note on domain options).
