The fastest new clinic in Canada arrives on wheels.

Proven in Canada

Provinces already run mobile health at scale

This isn't a pilot-project idea. Mobile clinical delivery is load-bearing infrastructure in Canadian healthcare today.

British Columbia

~10% of ALL screening mammograms in BC happen on mobile coaches

BC Cancer's three mobile mammography units visit 170+ rural communities — including 40+ First Nations communities — every year.

British Columbia

Mobile MRI & CT trailers booked six months out

The provincial health authority rotates two mobile imaging trailers among hospitals on a reservation system — demand outstrips the fleet, and sites plan deployments half a year ahead.

Alberta

120+ rural & Indigenous communities screened every year — since 1991

Alberta's Screen Test program operates 53-foot mobile mammography trailers, with two brand-new foundation-funded units delivered in 2025. Over 500,000 mammograms and counting.

Saskatchewan

Health authorities already lease mobile units

The Saskatchewan Health Authority leases the Regina General Hospital's mobile MRI from a third-party, Indigenous-owned provider — the exact partnership model MRA offers Canadian health systems.

British Columbia

A 24/7-ready "hospital on wheels"

BC keeps a Mobile Medical Unit deployment-ready around the clock for emergencies, facility backstops and clinical education — proof that standby mobile capacity is worth funding.
