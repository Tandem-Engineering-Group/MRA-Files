Why Mobile Healthcare

Canada's care gap is a distance problem.

Millions of Canadians live far from the services they need. Mobile clinical units close that distance — bringing screening, diagnostics and primary care to the community, instead of asking the community to travel.

6.5M+

Canadians without a regular primary-care provider

Mobile primary-care and outreach units extend clinician reach into communities with no permanent clinic.

~18%

of Canadians live in rural & remote communities

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you service units you didn't build?
Yes. Our maintenance and repair packages cover mobile medical trailers and coaches of any make — we inspect, document and maintain the full platform: chassis, generator, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, slide systems and seals.
Can you move a unit between provinces?
That's our specialty. The operational package includes an MRA CDL-A driver, the right tow vehicle, fuel, insurance, permit management across jurisdictions, storage between locations, full transport prep and placement at the host site.
We don't own a unit — can we still run a mobile program?
Yes. Our lease & operations package provides a fully customizable medical trailer plus the operational program to run it, so your team can focus on care while we handle everything mechanical and logistical.
What kinds of programs do your units support?
Diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT, X-ray), mammography and cancer screening, primary and urgent care, dental, mental health and addictions outreach, blood collection, vaccination clinics and community health promotion — if it can be delivered in a clinical room, it can usually be delivered on wheels.
Which regions do you cover?
All of Canada. MRA has supported healthcare organizations from Ontario and Manitoba to Saskatchewan and beyond since 1989, with cross-border capability through our North American operations.
