Case Study · Ontario 🍁

Windsor-Essex OHT Mobile Clinic

Community Health · Canada

A clinic that comes to the neighbourhood

The Windsor-Essex Ontario Health Team runs a full-size mobile clinic delivering testing, health services and supports directly to communities across the region — with a fully bilingual wrap (Mobile. Medical. Support. / Mobile. Médical. Soutien.) so the program speaks to everyone it serves.

It's exactly the model this site is about: the health team owns the clinical program; the platform, reliability and road are handled for them.

FormatStraight-truck mobile clinic
ProgramTesting · health services · supports
RegionWindsor-Essex, Ontario
Community Presence

Part of the community, not just parked in it

The same unit rolling in a Windsor parade under the Canadian flag — because a mobile clinic that communities recognize is a mobile clinic communities actually use. Program visibility is part of the design: full-wrap graphics, QR codes for booking, and a presence at community events between clinical stops.

Case Study · Clinical Training

A surgical suite on eighteen wheels

Medical Training · Device Demo

The OR that tours the country

A full operating-room environment — surgical lights, equipment booms, OR table, imaging displays and interactive touchscreen training walls — built into an expandable trailer so clinical teams can train on real equipment without leaving their hospital's parking lot.

For device manufacturers, the same platform puts products in front of surgeons at every stop on a national route; for health systems, it delivers hands-on education without travel budgets.

Case Study · Diagnostics Roadshow

The lab that visits the lab

Product Demo · Roadshow

Clinical chemistry, delivered to the loading dock

An expandable diagnostics showroom that tours hospital laboratories — working analyzers, connected displays and consult space inside; deployed stairs, awnings and full branding outside. Lab teams walk through the future of their own department without a flight or a conference fee.

MRA handles the route: transport between cities, site placement, power, and the maintenance that keeps a rolling laboratory calibrated and climate-controlled.
