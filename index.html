A4 Occupancy-Triggered Pricing Rule
Never underprice when demand is high; never lose tenants when slow Policy Rule
The rule: Write this policy on a sticky note and put it somewhere visible:

If occupancy > 90% → raise asking rate on next vacancy by $25–$50/wk. Demand is signaling you're underpriced.

If occupancy < 80% for 4+ weeks → activate the counter-promo bundle (2 weeks free + Vagaro setup). Do not drop rates permanently — use a short-term incentive only.

If you have a waitlist → you're underpriced. Raise rates immediately on all new leases.

Data: SBJC brand reports 92% renewal rate system-wide. Industry average for mature locations is 85–92% occupancy. You should be targeting 90%+ as baseline, not ceiling.

Peak Season (May–Dec)
At >93% occupancy, raise entry rates $25–50. Post a waitlist on social. This is when Peerspace after-hours events generate the most bookings.
