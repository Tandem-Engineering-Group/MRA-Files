How this works

This is the replacement for the parts of MyMobileTour you still use — the calendar / Add Event and the Affiliation Report (time, mileage & generator hours). Nothing here touches the old site.

Three tabs

SCHEDULE — add & edit tours/events, assign crew and equipment.
HOURS — log labor hours, vehicle mileage, and generator runtime against each event.
EQUIPMENT — manage the truck / vehicle / generator list and the crew roster.

Editing

Hit ＋ Add or the ✎ pencil on any item. A window pops up, you type, you Save — just like you pictured.

Right now this is a prototype. Your edits save only in this browser (so you can try it without anything going live). The next step is wiring Save to a shared store on your Azure/Microsoft 365 — then every team member sees the same data and the dashboard stays current for everyone, and it can still be opened as a file. Tell me what to add/change and I'll fold it in before we go live.
